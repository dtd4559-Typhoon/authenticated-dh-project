## Timing variation

Ranges and means below describe these runs, not guaranteed runtimes.

- 16 bits, prime_generation_s: min 0.000220500, mean 0.000247000, max 0.000274300 seconds.
- 16 bits, factorization_s: min 0.000001800, mean 0.000002933, max 0.000003900 seconds.
- 16 bits, generator_search_s: min 0.000017000, mean 0.000017667, max 0.000018000 seconds.
- 24 bits, prime_generation_s: min 0.000428600, mean 0.000441833, max 0.000449300 seconds.
- 24 bits, factorization_s: min 0.000004900, mean 0.000029767, max 0.000073000 seconds.
- 24 bits, generator_search_s: min 0.000016400, mean 0.000038833, max 0.000078100 seconds.
- 32 bits, prime_generation_s: min 0.000541400, mean 0.000660967, max 0.000897500 seconds.
- 32 bits, factorization_s: min 0.000197900, mean 0.000225900, max 0.000276600 seconds.
- 32 bits, generator_search_s: min 0.000226600, mean 0.000252800, max 0.000297800 seconds.

Library prime generation varies with random candidates; factorization varies with p - 1; generator search varies with the first passing candidate. Timer resolution, scheduling, and cache effects also matter. This small sample is not a reliable scaling study.

Factorization and generator search are measured as separate stages. The generator function independently recomputes the complete factors for defensive checking, so its duration includes that verification. Bob's independent received-parameter validation occurs in each exchange but is not included in Alice's parameter-generation stage timings.

See environment.json for the exact Python, OS, processor, and dependency versions.
